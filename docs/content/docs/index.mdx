---
title: "Introduction"
description: "Heimdall is the authority of an EUNOMIA data space: it decides who belongs and gives them credentials to prove it."
---

A data space needs someone who says who its participants are. **Heimdall** is that role. A participant asks for a credential, proves who it is, and receives a verifiable credential that other participants can check on their own, without calling the authority again.

It is built on open standards:

| Standard | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| [GNAP](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9635) | Credential requests, signed with HTTP Message Signatures |
| [OID4VP](https://openid.net/specs/openid-4-verifiable-presentations-1_0.html) | Presentations of the credentials a holder already has |
| [OID4VCI](https://openid.net/specs/openid-4-verifiable-credential-issuance-1_0.html) | Delivery of the credential to the holder's wallet |
| [W3C Verifiable Credentials](https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-data-model-2.0/) | The credentials themselves |
| `did:jwk`, `did:web` | Identifiers of the authority and its participants |

Heimdall is one piece of EUNOMIA. Participants run [DS-Agent](https://github.com/EunomiaUPM/ds-agent), keys and credentials live in [Fafnir](https://github.com/EunomiaUPM/fafnir-wallet), and the [EUNOMIA documentation](https://eunomiaupm.github.io/eunomia-deployment) explains how they work together.
