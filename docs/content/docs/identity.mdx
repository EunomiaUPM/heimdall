title: "Identity"

There are no shared accounts in a data space. Each participant has its own keys, and the authority links them to facts about that participant with credentials that anyone can verify.

## The identity of the authority

The authority has a DID and a key pair, both kept in its Fafnir wallet. They are created when the wallet is linked, and `did_config` chooses the method:

| Method | What it is | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `did:jwk` | The identifier is the public key, encoded. Nothing to resolve | Local instances without a domain |
| `did:web` | The identifier points to a document on a domain, served at `/.well-known/did.json` | Deployments with a domain. The quickstart uses it on `host.docker.internal` |

Its DID document also says how to reach it, with two services:

| Service | Points to |
| --- | --- |
| `CredentialIssuer` | `/api/v1/gate/access` |
| `FederatedCatalog` | `/.well-known/federated-catalog` |

## The identity of a participant

A participant identifies itself in two ways during a request:

- **Its request key.** The GNAP grant is signed with this key, so the authority knows the request comes from whoever holds it.
- **Its DID.** The credential is issued to this holder DID, and the wallet proves it controls that DID when it redeems the offer.

A certificate or a presentation then adds the facts behind them, depending on the [path of the request](/docs/architecture/issuance#2-prove).

## Credentials

| Credential | Issued by the role | Based on | Proves |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `DataSpaceParticipant` | `DataSpaceAuthority` | The request | Membership of the data space |
| `gx:VatId`, `gx:TaxId`, `gx:LeiCode`, `gx:LocalRegistrationNumber` | `LegalAuthority` | The X.509 certificate | A legal identifier of the holder |
| `gx:LabelCredential` | `ClearingHouse` | A presentation | That the holder presented the three credentials below |

`EcoAuthority` issues all of them.

### Legal credentials

The value comes from the certificate of the participant: the country from its subject, and the identifier from `organizationIdentifier`, in the ETSI format. A VAT number looks like `VATES-123456789`.

| Credential | Accepted prefixes |
| --- | --- |
| `gx:VatId` | `VAT` |
| `gx:TaxId` | `TIN`, `VAT` |
| `gx:LeiCode` | `LEI` |
| `gx:LocalRegistrationNumber` | `NTR` |

### Label credential

To issue it, the authority needs exactly three presented credentials: a `gx:LegalPerson`, a `gx:TermsAndConditions` and one legal identifier. The label lists them, with the level and versions set in `issue_config.clh_config`.

<Callout type="warn" title="Not a Gaia-X credential">
  The `ClearingHouse` role [imitates what a Gaia-X Digital Clearing House does](/docs/architecture#clearinghouse). The `gx:` names follow the Gaia-X vocabulary, but this authority signs these credentials itself, and v1.5.1 does not claim conformance with Gaia-X.
</Callout>

## Secrets

Keys and certificates never go in the configuration. The authority reads them from HashiCorp Vault, or from `vault/secrets` when `is_vault_real` is `false`. [Production](/docs/production) lists what each one is for.
